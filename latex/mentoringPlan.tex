\noindent \textbf{Mentoring Plan}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent The project supports one postdoctoral researcher, co-advised by
PI Napolitano and Co-PI Wauthier (SAR processing and Bayesian fusion), and one
PhD student, advised by Co-PI Busse and co-mentored by all PIs (mass balance
modeling and community co-development). All PIs are at Penn State, enabling
continuous cross-group engagement rather than periodic visits.
Both mentees receive cross-disciplinary training spanning SAR imagery processing,
hydrological mass balance, probabilistic structural assessment, and
community-engaged research. The plan will be updated annually based on mentee
feedback and assessed through progress reports and professional achievements.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Regular meetings and cross-disciplinary co-mentoring:}
PI Napolitano and Co-PI Wauthier will hold weekly meetings with the postdoc;
Co-PI Busse weekly with the PhD student. Monthly cross-team meetings ensure
integration across the SAR, hydrological, and co-design components. Both mentees
attend lab meetings across all three groups: the postdoc joins Busse's mass
balance meetings; the PhD student works with Napolitano on fragility surfaces and
attends Wauthier's SAR sessions. Both participate in Napolitano's annual community
co-development workshops in Montpelier.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Career counseling and professional development:}
Mentees will present at conferences (AGU, ASCE EMI, Association for Preservation
Technology) and submit to peer-reviewed journals. Each PI provides career counseling
on academic, industry, and government pathways.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Grant writing and scholarly communication:}
Training in preparing NSF proposals, publications, and presentations through Penn
State's Graduate Writing Center. PI Napolitano will share materials from her
graduate research methods course.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Teaching and mentoring skill development:}
Mentees will assist in developing project-related course content. The PhD student
will be encouraged to pursue the Penn State CIRTL Certificate and will receive
mentorship on supervising undergraduates through the Penn State Undergraduate
Research and Fellowships Mentoring program.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Undergraduate research:}
Two undergraduates per year participate through the BESURE program at Penn State,
with recruitment prioritizing underrepresented groups via the WISER and MURE
programs.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Diversity training and responsible practices:}
All mentees participate in DEI training at Penn State and receive responsible
conduct of research training through Penn State's SARI program, covering research
ethics, data management, and intellectual property.
