\noindent \textbf{Mentoring Plan}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent The project supports one postdoctoral researcher for four years,
co-advised by PI Napolitano (structural vulnerability and community co-design),
Co-PI Wauthier (SAR processing and uncertainty quantification), and
Co-PI Busse (hydrological modeling and mass balance). All PIs are at Penn State,
enabling continuous cross-group engagement rather than periodic visits.
The postdoc spans the project's technical lanes, implementing SAR data processing
and Bayesian fusion under Wauthier's direction, building the mass balance model
under Busse's direction, and contributing to fragility calibration and community
interface development under Napolitano's direction.
The postdoc receives cross-disciplinary training spanning SAR imagery processing,
hydrological mass balance modeling, probabilistic structural assessment, and
community-engaged research. The plan will be updated annually based on mentee
feedback and assessed through progress reports and professional achievements.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Regular meetings and cross-disciplinary co-mentoring:}
PI Napolitano serves as primary advisor and holds weekly meetings with the postdoc.
Co-PIs Wauthier and Busse each hold biweekly one-on-one meetings aligned with
their technical lanes. Monthly cross-team meetings ensure integration across
the SAR, hydrological, and co-design components. The postdoc attends lab meetings
across all three groups: Wauthier's SAR sessions, Busse's mass balance meetings,
and Napolitano's structural assessment and community engagement meetings.
The postdoc participates in Napolitano's annual community co-development workshops
in Montpelier during Years~1--3.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Career counseling and professional development:}
The postdoc will present at conferences (AGU, ASCE EMI, Association for Preservation
Technology) and submit to peer-reviewed journals. Each PI provides career counseling
on academic, industry, and government pathways.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Grant writing and scholarly communication:}
Training in preparing NSF proposals, publications, and presentations through Penn
State's Graduate Writing Center. PI Napolitano will share materials from her
graduate research methods course. The postdoc will develop at least one independent
funding application during the appointment.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Teaching and mentoring skill development:}
The postdoc will co-supervise BESURE undergraduate researchers each year,
developing supervision and mentoring skills. The postdoc will receive
mentorship on undergraduate supervision through the Penn State Undergraduate
Research and Fellowships Mentoring program and is encouraged to guest lecture
in PI courses and attend pedagogy workshops through the CIRTL Network.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Undergraduate research:}
Two undergraduates per year participate through the BESURE program at Penn State,
with recruitment prioritizing underrepresented groups via the WISER and MURE
programs. The postdoc serves as day-to-day mentor for these students.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Diversity training and responsible practices:}
The postdoc participates in DEI training at Penn State and completes responsible
conduct of research training through Penn State's SARI program, covering research
ethics, data management, and intellectual property.
